\section*{Solution: the exponential-spectator obstruction}
\label{sec:sol-weighted-spectator-obstruction}

\paragraph{Conventions.}
Let $\lambda$ be the law of $Y-1$, where $Y$ is rate-one exponential.  Thus $\lambda$ is
centered, has variance one, and has density
\[
  w_1(x)=e^{-(x+1)}\one_{\{x\ge-1\}}.
\]
All perimeters below use the outer Minkowski convention of the manuscript.  For the regular
sets selected below this agrees with relative weighted BV perimeter inside the convex support.
For a localized measure $\mu_t$, a fixed cut $E$, and
$p_t=\mu_t(E)$, write
\[
  P_t(E)=\mu_t^+(E),\qquad
  e_t(E)=P_t(E)-I_{\mu_t}(p_t),\qquad
  \tau_\eta=\inf\{t:|p_t-\tfrac12|>\eta\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exponential-spectator obstruction; Proposition~\ref{prop:weighted-spectator-obstruction}]
\label{thm:sol-weighted-spectator-obstruction}
For every $C,T_0,\gamma>0$, every $\eta\in(0,1/2)$, and every $\delta>0$, there are an
integer $d$, the isotropic log-concave product $\mu=\lambda^{\otimes d}$, a finite-perimeter
cylinder $E\subset\R^d$ with $\mu(E)=1/2$ and
\[
  e_0(E)\le\delta,
  \qquad
  \frac{e_0(E)}{I_\mu(1/2)}\le\delta,
\]
and a time $0<T\le T_0$ such that
\begin{equation}
  \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}
  e_t(E)\bigl(1+\norm{A_t}_\op\bigr)^{5/2}\dd t
  >C\bigl(Te_0(E)+T^{1+\gamma}\bigr).
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-violation}
\end{equation}
The construction can additionally be made with $e_0(E)\le1$.  Hence it refutes the current
literal global-operator-norm weighted gate in Question~\ref{q:weighted}, including both the
additive and relative approximate-minimizer readings.  It does not refute KLS: every witness is
a product measure and has a dimension-free KLS constant by Proposition~\ref{prop:products}.
\end{theorem}

We first isolate the regularization convention used to select the base cut.  This also records
why the later perimeter change-of-density formula is valid for the exact, noncompact
one-sided-exponential law, rather than only for a compact surrogate.

\begin{lemma}[Regular competitors and the Minkowski convention]
\label{lem:sol-spectator-regularization}
Let $\nu=\lambda^{\otimes m}$ and let $p\in(0,1)$.  For every $\rho>0$ there is a relative
weighted-BV set $S$ of mass $p$, regular in the interior of
$K_m=[-1,\infty)^m$, such that
\[
  \nu^+(S)\le I_\nu(p)+\rho.
\]
For this $S$, outer Minkowski perimeter equals relative weighted perimeter.  For parameters
$(u,t)$ such that $t\ge0$ and
\[
  0<Z(u,t):=\int\exp(u\cdot x-t|x|^2/2)\dd\nu(x)<\infty,
\]
define
\[
  \dd\nu_{u,t}=F_{u,t}\dd\nu,
  \qquad
  F_{u,t}(x)=\frac{\exp(u\cdot x-t|x|^2/2)}{Z(u,t)},
\]
Then
\begin{equation}
  \nu_{u,t}^+(S)=\int F_{u,t}\,\dd\sigma_S,
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-density-change}
\end{equation}
where
$\dd\sigma_S=e^{-\sum_{j=1}^m(x_j+1)}
\dd\mathcal H^{m-1}|_{\partial^*S\cap\operatorname{int}K_m}$ is its relative weighted
perimeter measure.  No surface term on $\partial K_m$ occurs.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Here are the approximation details needed later.  On each compact subset of
$\operatorname{int}K_m$, the density of $\nu$ is smooth and strictly positive.  Start from a
mass-$p$ competitor $S^{(0)}$ whose outer Minkowski content is within $\rho/4$ of $I_\nu(p)$.
Finite outer Minkowski content implies finite relative BV perimeter, with BV perimeter no larger
than that content.  Restrict to $K_m\cap B_R$, mollify the indicator in interior and boundary
charts, and use the coarea formula; then let $R\uparrow\infty$.  The usual diagonal choice gives
regular relative sets $S_k$ with
\[
  \nu(S_k)\longrightarrow p,
  \qquad
  \operatorname{Per}_\nu(S_k)\longrightarrow
  \operatorname{Per}_\nu(S^{(0)}).
\]
If necessary, a compactly supported smooth flow through a regular boundary patch in
$\operatorname{int}K_m$ corrects the small mass error exactly.  Its mass derivative is the
strictly positive weighted area of that patch, while its perimeter varies continuously, so the
correction costs $o(1)$ perimeter.  This is the compact-smooth weighted-BV approximation layer;
choose $k$ large and rename one exact-mass corrected regular approximant as $S$.  It gives the
displayed $\rho$ bound.

For a regular relative set, the one-sided tubular-neighborhood formula inside $K_m$ identifies
outer Minkowski content with
\[
  \int_{\partial^*S\cap\operatorname{int}K_m}
       e^{-\sum_j(x_j+1)}\dd\mathcal H^{m-1}.
\]
Parts of the topological boundary lying on $\partial K_m$ are not interfaces inside the support
and contribute neither to this formula nor to the ambient Minkowski derivative, since the
measure vanishes outside $K_m$.  Multiplying the volume density by the positive continuous
factor $F_{u,t}$ leaves the relative reduced boundary unchanged and multiplies its weighted
surface measure by $F_{u,t}$.  Applying the same tubular-neighborhood formula proves
\eqref{eq:sol-spectator-density-change}.  This direct weighted-BV identity is valid for the
exact exponential product; no compact approximation of the probability law is taken in the
argument below.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:sol-weighted-spectator-obstruction}]
\emph{Step 1: choose a base before choosing either time or spectator dimension.}
Set
\[
  a_m:=I_{\lambda^{\otimes m}}(1/2),\qquad m\ge1.
\]
Cylinder extension makes this sequence nonincreasing.  Indeed, if
$S\subset\R^m$ has mass $1/2$, then
\[
  \dist\bigl((x,z),S\times\R\bigr)=\dist(x,S),
  \qquad
  (S\times\R)_r=S_r\times\R,
\]
so $\lambda^{\otimes(m+1)}(S\times\R)=1/2$ and its outer Minkowski perimeter equals
$\lambda^{\otimes m,+}(S)$.  Taking the infimum gives $a_{m+1}\le a_m$.

On the other hand, Proposition~\ref{prop:products} gives a universal $h_*>0$ such that
$h_{\lambda^{\otimes m}}\ge h_*$ for every $m$: every factor is isotropic and
one-dimensional.  Lemma~\ref{lem:half} therefore gives
\begin{equation}
  a_m=\frac12h_{\lambda^{\otimes m}}\ge a_*:=\frac{h_*}{2}>0.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-profile-floor}
\end{equation}
Consequently
\[
  a_m\downarrow a_\infty\quad\hbox{with}\quad a_\infty\ge a_*>0.
\]

Choose $\varepsilon>0$ so small that
\begin{equation}
  2\varepsilon\le\min\{\delta,1,\delta a_*\}.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-epsilon}
\end{equation}
First choose $M$ with $a_M-a_\infty\le\varepsilon/2$.  Then apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:sol-spectator-regularization} with $p=1/2$ and $\rho=\varepsilon/2$ to choose
a regular relative finite-perimeter set $E_0\subset\R^M$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
  \lambda^{\otimes M}(E_0)=\frac12,
  \qquad
  P_0:=\lambda^{\otimes M,+}(E_0)\le a_M+\frac\varepsilon2.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-base}
\end{equation}
By the definition of the profile and \eqref{eq:sol-spectator-profile-floor},
$P_0\ge a_M\ge a_*>0$.  For every integer $N\ge1$, put
\[
  \mu^{M,N}=\lambda^{\otimes M}\otimes\lambda^{\otimes N},
  \qquad
  E^{M,N}=E_0\times\R^N.
\]
The distance identity above gives mass $1/2$ and perimeter $P_0$.  Hence
\begin{align}
  0\le e_0(E^{M,N})
  &=P_0-a_{M+N}
    \le P_0-a_\infty\le\varepsilon,
    \label{eq:sol-spectator-additive}\\
  \frac{e_0(E^{M,N})}{I_{\mu^{M,N}}(1/2)}
  &\le\frac{\varepsilon}{a_*}.
    \label{eq:sol-spectator-relative}
\end{align}
In particular, \eqref{eq:sol-spectator-epsilon} makes both quantities at most $\delta$ and
makes the additive excess at most $1$.  Most importantly, these bounds are uniform in every
spectator dimension $N$ chosen later.

\emph{Step 2: product localization and the independent base event.}
Use the planted Gaussian observation realization.  Write
\[
  X=(X^0,X^1,\ldots,X^N),
  \qquad
  c_t=tX+B_t,
\]
where $X^0\sim\lambda^{\otimes M}$, $X^i\sim\lambda$ for $i\ge1$, and all latent blocks and
Brownian coordinate blocks are independent.  Conditional on the observation path up to time
$t$, its endpoint $c_t$ is sufficient and
\[
  \dd\mu_t(x)=Z_t^{-1}
  \exp\bigl(c_t\cdot x-t|x|^2/2\bigr)\dd\mu^{M,N}(x).
\]
Both the prior and the likelihood factorize, so, pathwise,
\begin{equation}
  \mu_t=\nu_t^0\otimes\bigotimes_{i=1}^N\lambda_{i,t},
  \qquad
  A_t=\operatorname{diag}(A_t^0,v_{1,t},\ldots,v_{N,t}),
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-factorization}
\end{equation}
where $v_{i,t}=\Var(\lambda_{i,t})$.  The entire base posterior process is independent of the
spectator posterior processes.  The cylinder and distance identities give, for every $t$,
\begin{equation}
  p_t=\nu_t^0(E_0),
  \qquad
  P_t(E^{M,N})=(\nu_t^0)^+(E_0).
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-cylinder-localized}
\end{equation}
Thus $p_t$, $P_t(E^{M,N})$, and $\tau_\eta$ are base-block measurable.

We next construct a single base event on which mass and perimeter are stable for a short time.
For deterministic $(u,t)$ let
\[
  F_{u,t}(x)=
  \frac{\exp(u\cdot x-t|x|^2/2)}
       {\int\exp(u\cdot y-t|y|^2/2)\dd\lambda^{\otimes M}(y)}.
\]
The exponential product has a local exponential moment.  Dominated convergence, applied
uniformly as $(a,\bar T)\downarrow(0,0)$, therefore permits $a,\bar T>0$ such that
\begin{equation}
  \int e^{a|x|}\dd\lambda^{\otimes M}(x)\le2,
  \qquad
  \sup_{|u|\le a,\ 0\le t\le\bar T}
  \norm{F_{u,t}-1}_{L^1(\lambda^{\otimes M})}<\eta.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-l1-stability}
\end{equation}
For completeness, the uniform domination is by $1+e^{a_0|x|}$ for a fixed sufficiently small
local-moment radius $a_0$, while
$\sup_{|u|\le a,\ 0\le t\le\bar T}|e^{u\cdot x-t|x|^2/2}-1|\to0$ pointwise.

Let $\sigma_0$ be the finite weighted perimeter measure of $E_0$.  Choose $R<\infty$ so that
\begin{equation}
  \sigma_0(B_R)\ge\frac{P_0}{2},
  \qquad
  aR+\frac{\bar T R^2}{2}\le\log2,
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-R}
\end{equation}
shrinking $a,\bar T$ again if required; the $L^1$ conclusion in
\eqref{eq:sol-spectator-l1-stability} is preserved.  Choose $L$ with
$\Prob(|X^0|\le L)\ge1/2$, and take
\begin{equation}
  T_b\le
  \min\left\{\bar T,\frac{a}{2L},
  \frac{a^2}{8M\log(8M)}\right\}.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-Tb}
\end{equation}
Define the base-block event
\begin{equation}
  G_b=\{|X^0|\le L\}\cap
  \left\{
    \max_{1\le j\le M}\sup_{0\le s\le T_b}|B_s^{0,j}|
    \le\frac{a}{2\sqrt M}
  \right\}.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-Gb}
\end{equation}
The reflection principle and a union bound give
\[
  \Prob\left(
    \max_j\sup_{s\le T_b}|B_s^{0,j}|>\frac{a}{2\sqrt M}
  \right)
  \le4M\exp\left(-\frac{a^2}{8MT_b}\right)\le\frac12.
\]
Independence of $X^0$ and $B^0$ yields $\Prob(G_b)\ge1/4$.  On $G_b$,
$|c_s^0|=|sX^0+B_s^0|\le a$ for all $s\le T_b$.  The second part of
\eqref{eq:sol-spectator-l1-stability} then gives
\[
  |\nu_s^0(E_0)-\tfrac12|<\eta.
\]
Moreover, for $x\in B_R$, the first part of
\eqref{eq:sol-spectator-l1-stability} and \eqref{eq:sol-spectator-R} give
\[
  F_{c_s^0,s}(x)
  \ge
  \frac{e^{-aR-T_bR^2/2}}
       {\int e^{a|y|}\dd\lambda^{\otimes M}(y)}
  \ge\frac14.
\]
The exact density-change formula \eqref{eq:sol-spectator-density-change} therefore yields,
simultaneously for every $0\le s\le T_b$,
\begin{equation}
  \tau_\eta>T_b,
  \qquad
  P_s(E^{M,N})=(\nu_s^0)^+(E_0)
  \ge\frac14\sigma_0(B_R)\ge\frac{P_0}{8}
  \quad\hbox{on }G_b.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-base-stability}
\end{equation}
Neither $G_b$ nor $T_b$ depends on the later choice of $N$.

\emph{Step 3: a fixed-time spectator spike and an exact-mass profile competitor.}
The imported covariance-spike proposition, in its event form, says that for $N$ independent
centered rate-one exponentials and Gaussian-channel noise variance $s$,
\begin{equation}
  \Prob\left(
    \norm{\Cov(X\mid X+\sqrt sG)}_\op\ge c_{\rm sp}s
  \right)
  \ge1-\left(1-\frac12e^{-s}\right)^N
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-KL-channel}
\end{equation}
for a universal $c_{\rm sp}>0$; this is the event computed in
\cite[Prop.~65]{KLnotes}.  The localization observation satisfies
\[
  \frac{c_t}{t}=X+\frac{B_t}{t}
  \stackrel{d}=X+t^{-1/2}G.
\]
Thus the conversion is exactly $s=1/t$, and not $s=t$ or $s=t^{-1/2}$.  In the spectator block,
put
\[
  H_t=\left\{\max_{1\le i\le N}v_{i,t}\ge\frac{c_{\rm sp}}t\right\}.
\]
Equation~\eqref{eq:sol-spectator-KL-channel} gives, at each deterministic $t>0$,
\begin{equation}
  \Prob(H_t)\ge1-\left(1-\frac12e^{-1/t}\right)^N.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-fixed-time}
\end{equation}
If $\log N\ge2/T$, then, separately for every deterministic $t\in[T/2,T]$,
\begin{equation}
  \Prob(H_t)\ge h_{\rm sp}:=1-e^{-1/2}>0.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-fixed-time-positive}
\end{equation}
This is only a fixed-time assertion; no common spike event on the interval is claimed.

On $H_t$, take the least $i$ with $v_{i,t}\ge c_{\rm sp}/t$.  Since the one-dimensional
posterior $\lambda_{i,t}$ is log-concave and has a continuous positive density $f_{i,t}$ in
the interior of its support, it has a quantile $q_{i,t}$ satisfying
$\lambda_{i,t}(( -\infty,q_{i,t}])=p_t$.  The product cylinder cut in this one spectator
coordinate has $\mu_t$-mass exactly $p_t$ and outer Minkowski perimeter
$f_{i,t}(q_{i,t})$.  The imported sharp density--variance bound
\cite[Prop.~2.1]{BobkovChistyakov2015Concentration}, in the ledger form of
Lemma~\ref{lem:one-dimensional-density-variance}, gives
\begin{equation}
  I_{\mu_t}(p_t)\le f_{i,t}(q_{i,t})
  \le\norm{f_{i,t}}_\infty
  \le v_{i,t}^{-1/2}
  \le\sqrt{\frac{t}{c_{\rm sp}}}.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-profile-competitor}
\end{equation}
This is a pointwise upper bound on the profile at the actual random mass $p_t$.  It inserts no
lower bound on a localized profile.

\emph{Step 4: choose $T$, then $N$, and integrate fixed-time events by Tonelli.}
With the base, $P_0$, and $T_b$ already fixed, choose $T>0$ so small that
\begin{equation}
  T\le\min\left\{T_0,T_b,\frac{c_{\rm sp}P_0^2}{256},1\right\}.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-T-first}
\end{equation}
At this same choice, also require
\begin{equation}
  T^{5/2}<\frac{\kappa_{\rm sp}}{2C},
  \qquad
  T^{\gamma+5/2}<\frac{\kappa_{\rm sp}P_0}{2C},
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-final-T}
\end{equation}
where
\[
  \kappa_{\rm sp}
  :=\frac{h_{\rm sp}c_{\rm sp}^{5/2}}{96}
       (2^{3/2}-1)>0.
\]
Such a positive $T$ exists because every quantity on the right is already fixed.
Only now choose a finite integer $N$ with $\log N\ge2/T$.  For every deterministic
$t\in[T/2,T]$, on $G_b\cap H_t$, equations
\eqref{eq:sol-spectator-base-stability} and
\eqref{eq:sol-spectator-profile-competitor} imply
\begin{equation}
  t<\tau_\eta,
  \qquad
  e_t(E^{M,N})\ge\frac{P_0}{8}-\sqrt{\frac{t}{c_{\rm sp}}}
  \ge\frac{P_0}{16}.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-excess-lower}
\end{equation}
The block-diagonal covariance in \eqref{eq:sol-spectator-factorization} also gives
\begin{equation}
  \bigl(1+\norm{A_t}_\op\bigr)^{5/2}
  \ge c_{\rm sp}^{5/2}t^{-5/2}.
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-weight-lower}
\end{equation}
For each such deterministic $t$, $G_b$ is independent of $H_t$, because they depend on
disjoint latent and Brownian blocks.  Therefore
\[
  \Prob(G_b\cap H_t)\ge\frac{h_{\rm sp}}4.
\]
The integrand is nonnegative, so Tonelli applied to these marginal-in-time events gives
\begin{align}
  &\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}
     e_t(E^{M,N})\bigl(1+\norm{A_t}_\op\bigr)^{5/2}\dd t
  \notag\\
  &\quad\ge
  \frac{P_0h_{\rm sp}c_{\rm sp}^{5/2}}{64}
  \int_{T/2}^Tt^{-5/2}\dd t
  =\kappa_{\rm sp}P_0T^{-3/2},
  \label{eq:sol-spectator-integrated-lower}
\end{align}
No persistent spike and no interchange beyond nonnegative Tonelli is used.

Since $e_0(E^{M,N})\le P_0$, the conditions imposed before the single final choice of $N$ give
\[
  C\bigl(Te_0(E^{M,N})+T^{1+\gamma}\bigr)
  \le C\bigl(TP_0+T^{1+\gamma}\bigr)
  <\kappa_{\rm sp}P_0T^{-3/2}.
\]
Together with \eqref{eq:sol-spectator-integrated-lower}, this is
\eqref{eq:sol-spectator-violation}.  The quantifier order was
\[
  (C,T_0,\gamma,\eta,\delta)
  \longrightarrow(\varepsilon,M,E_0,P_0,T_b)
  \longrightarrow T
  \longrightarrow N,
\]
and the near-minimality estimates \eqref{eq:sol-spectator-additive}--
\eqref{eq:sol-spectator-relative} were uniform in that final $N$.  This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Dependency and fence audit.}
The proof uses exactly the four ledger dependencies in the header.  Proposition
\ref{prop:products} and Lemma~\ref{lem:half} keep the monotone half-profile limit positive;
Proposition~\ref{prop:covariance-spike} supplies only the fixed-time event
\eqref{eq:sol-spectator-fixed-time}; and
Lemma~\ref{lem:one-dimensional-density-variance} supplies the explicit quantile-halfline
competitor.  The node has no \texttt{bounded\_by} fence.  The neighbouring fences are nevertheless
respected: the proof retains the $5/2$ weight demanded by the two-tail calibration and evades
the circularity warning by upper-bounding the random posterior profile with an explicit set.
It asserts no implication about the trace-upgrade cluster.

\paragraph{Hypotheses and closure.}
All four dependencies are proved or published imports in the current ledger, so the result is
unconditional relative to the repository's accepted analytic inputs.  Besides them, the proof
uses only standard deterministic weighted-BV approximation and density change, elementary local
exponential integrability of a finite exponential product, the planted Gaussian posterior,
the Brownian reflection principle, and Tonelli's theorem.  There is no numerical input.  There
is no unclosed analytic step in this dossier; \texttt{checked\_by: none} records that a distinct
cold reviewer has not yet audited it.

\paragraph{What is and is not refuted.}
The construction refutes only the literal all-product, global-$\norm{A_t}_\op$ rate stated in
Question~\ref{q:weighted}.  A replacement with an explicit near-worst-measure condition, or a
cut-local tensor-stable covariance weight, is a different statement.  Since the witnesses
themselves satisfy dimension-free KLS, no counterexample to the KLS conjecture is claimed.
