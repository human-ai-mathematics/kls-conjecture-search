\section*{Solution: split-class screened weighted supply (total-budget form)}
\label{sec:sol-split-screened-supply}

\paragraph{Scope.}
This dossier proves the screened weighted-excess supply estimate for coordinate cuts on
products of isotropic one-dimensional log-concave measures, in \emph{total-budget} form: the
bound is a constant depending only on the number $k$ of active coordinates, the window
half-width $\eta$, and the screening ratio $\kappa$; it is uniform in the horizon $T$, in the
ambient dimension $n$, and in every spectator coordinate.  It is \textbf{not} an estimate of
the form $C(k)\,T$, and no claim of that fixed-time or linear-in-$T$ shape is made here.  The
dossier asserts nothing about the trace-upgrade cluster
(\ref{q:upgrade}, the high-rank part of \ref{q:stein-weighted}, \ref{q:alignment}); in
particular it neither proves nor compares any occupation estimate of that cluster, in
compliance with repository constraint~6.  A separate auxiliary lemma
(Lemma~\ref{lem:sol-constant-supply-consumption} below) records the constant-supply extension
of Corollary~\ref{cor:tight-window-consumption}; it is logically independent of the main
proposition and is clearly separated from it.

\paragraph{Regularity and measurability conventions.}
We adopt verbatim the conventions of Section~5 of the probe record
\begin{center}
\url{research/explorations/2026-08-30-kls-route-prober-weighted-screened-interface-w4w01.md}:
\end{center}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(M\arabic*),leftmargin=3em]
  \item We work on the usual $\Prob$-augmented right-continuous filtration of the driving
  $n$-dimensional Brownian motion of Eldan's stochastic localization.
  \item \emph{Regular split class.}  The main theorem is stated and proved for initial data
  in the compact-smooth product-preserving regularity class of the certified Riccati-core and
  excess-audit dossiers (\texttt{kls-localization-riccati-core.tex} and
  \texttt{kls-excess-audit.tex}, both under \texttt{solutions/}): each
  factor $\mu^{(i)}$ is a compactly supported
  one-dimensional log-concave law with smooth density, normalized to mean zero and variance
  one, and the cut $E=E_J\times\R^{J^c}$ is measurable with respect to the coordinates in $J$
  with $E_J\subset\R^{J}$ having $C^2$ relative boundary with a tubular neighborhood over the
  support, so that $P_0(E)<\infty$ and the one-sided tube formula identifies the lower outer
  Minkowski perimeter with weighted surface area.  On every bounded time interval all
  stochastic integrals below are true martingales after the usual bounded stopping, with
  regularization-independent constants.  Passage to general split laws and cuts is discussed,
  and its unresolved part explicitly flagged, in
  Remark~\ref{rem:sol-general-class} below.
  \item $P_t=P_t(E)=\mu_t^+(E)$ is the lower outer Minkowski perimeter, realized pathwise as
  the increasing limit of infima over the fixed countable rational boundary-layer family of
  bounded continuous mass martingales (Lemma~\ref{lem:perimeter-martingale}, construction of
  \texttt{solutions/kls-excess-audit.tex}); as a pathwise monotone limit of countable infima of
  continuous adapted processes it is progressively measurable.  In the regular class the
  localized density field is jointly measurable and positive, and the moving profile value
  $I_{\mu_t}(p_t)$ is jointly measurable in $(t,\omega)$ via a fixed countable regular
  competitor family; hence the excess
  \[
    e_t \;=\; e_t(E)\;=\;P_t(E)-I_{\mu_t}(p_t)
  \]
  is progressively measurable.  This is a \emph{measurability} convention only: no
  supermartingale or martingale property of the moving profile infimum is asserted
  (the caveat of Lemma~\ref{lem:inf-martingales} is preserved), and no lower bound on
  $I_{\mu_t}(p_t)$ is used anywhere in this dossier.
  \item On $\{0<p_t<1\}$ the processes $A_t$, $K_t$, $Q_t$ below are continuous and adapted in
  the regular class, and the map $(A,K)\mapsto\lambda_{\rm cut}(A,K)$ is Borel on supported
  pairs $\{(A,K):A\succeq0,\ K=P_{\operatorname{Ran}A}\,K\,P_{\operatorname{Ran}A}\}$
  (fixed-rank strata together with the Borel Moore--Penrose formula
  \eqref{eq:sol-lyapunov-pseudoinverse} of
  \texttt{solutions/lem-lyapunov-stein-duality.tex}, with the separate convention
  $\lambda_{\rm cut}(A,0)=0$).  Hence the weight $W_{\rm cut}(A_t,K_t)$ and the screened
  indicator
  $\one_{\mathcal A_{\kappa,t}}=\one_{\{Q_t-\kappa e_tW_{\rm cut}(A_t,K_t)\ge0\}}$ are
  progressively measurable.  Both appear \emph{only} inside nonnegative Lebesgue-time
  integrals, handled by Tonelli; no It\^o differential is ever taken of the weight, of the
  indicator, or of any function of $\lambda_{\rm cut}$.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Notation.}
Fix the manuscript localization notation: $p_t=\mu_t(E)$, $q_t=1-p_t$, $s_t=p_tq_t$,
$\delta_t=m_t^E-m_t^F$, $G_t=\Sigma_t^E-\Sigma_t^F$,
$K_t=G_t+(q_t-p_t)\delta_t\delta_t^T$,
$B_t=s_t\delta_t\delta_t^T$, $R_t=A_t-B_t$, $r_t=\Tr B_t=s_t\abs{\delta_t}^2$,
$S_t=s_t\norm{G_t}_\HS^2$, and $D_t=2s_t\delta_t^TA_t\delta_t-r_t^2\ge r_t^2\ge0$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:scalar-riccati}).  Set
\[
  Q_t\;=\;s_t\norm{K_t}_\HS^2\;=\;\frac{\calS_{\mu_t}(E)}{s_t},
\]
the second equality being the certified Stein-norm identity
$\calS_\nu(E)=s^2\norm K_\HS^2$ of Proposition~\ref{prop:stein-rep}.  With the convention
$\lambda_{\rm cut}(A,0)=0$ of Lemma~\ref{lem:lyapunov-stein-duality}, put
\[
  W_{\rm cut}(A,K)\;=\;\bigl(1+\lambda_{\rm cut}(A,K)\bigr)^{5/2},
  \qquad
  \mathcal A_{\kappa,t}\;=\;\bigl\{Q_t\ \ge\ \kappa\,e_t\,W_{\rm cut}(A_t,K_t)\bigr\}.
\]
Finally, for $\eta\in(0,1/4]$ let
$\tau_\eta=\inf\{t\ge0:\abs{p_t-1/2}>\eta\}$ be the continuous-exit time of
\texttt{solutions/kls-localization-riccati-core.tex}; it is a stopping time for the augmented
right-continuous filtration because $p$ has continuous paths.

\paragraph{Refined statement.}
The theorem below is the verbatim statement of the candidate ledger node
\texttt{prop:split-screened-supply}, restricted per convention (M2) to the regular split
class; Remark~\ref{rem:sol-general-class} records the approximant convention for general split
laws together with the explicit unresolved limit-interchange gap.

\begin{theorem}[split-class screened weighted supply, total-budget form]
\label{thm:sol-split-screened-supply}
Let $\mu=\bigotimes_{i=1}^n\mu^{(i)}$ be a product of isotropic one-dimensional log-concave
probability measures in the regular split class (M2), let $J\subset\{1,\dots,n\}$ with
$\abs J=k$, and let $E$ be measurable with respect to the coordinates in $J$ with
$0<p_0=\mu(E)<1$.  Then, for every $\eta\in(0,1/4]$, every $\kappa>0$, and every $T>0$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sol-split-screened-supply}
  \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}
    e_t\,\bigl(1+\lambda_{\rm cut}(A_t,K_t)\bigr)^{5/2}\,
    \one_{\{Q_t\ \ge\ \kappa\,e_t\,W_{\rm cut}(A_t,K_t)\}}\,\dd t
  \;\le\;\frac{2k+64\,\eta^2(1+k)}{\kappa}.
\end{equation}
The right-hand side is independent of $T$, of the ambient dimension $n$, and of every
spectator factor $\mu^{(i)}$, $i\notin J$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Throughout, product structure is preserved pathwise by stochastic localization and the
posterior covariance $A_t$ is diagonal (Proposition~\ref{prop:products}); the fixed cut $E$
remains $J$-measurable; and, since $0<p_0<1$ and the finite-time posterior has a strictly
positive likelihood relative to $\mu$, we have $0<p_t<1$ at every finite time, so all the
displayed two-color quantities are defined along the whole path.  If
$\abs{p_0-1/2}>\eta$ then $\tau_\eta=0$ and \eqref{eq:sol-split-screened-supply} is the
trivial statement $0\le(2k+64\eta^2(1+k))/\kappa$; assume therefore
$\abs{p_0-1/2}\le\eta$ from now on.  All integrands below are nonnegative and progressively
measurable by conventions (M3)--(M4), so every interchange of $\E$ and $\dd t$ is Tonelli for
nonnegative integrands and needs no a priori integrability.

\medskip
\emph{Step 1: pathwise domination on the aligned set.}
We claim that, pathwise, for every $t$ with $0<p_t<1$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sol-screen-domination}
  e_t\,W_{\rm cut}(A_t,K_t)\,\one_{\mathcal A_{\kappa,t}}
  \;\le\;\frac{Q_t}{\kappa}.
\end{equation}
First, $e_t\ge0$: the set $E$ has $\mu_t$-mass $p_t$ and perimeter $P_t(E)$, hence is a
competitor in the infimum defining the profile value $I_{\mu_t}(p_t)$, so
$I_{\mu_t}(p_t)\le P_t(E)$.  This uses only the definition of the profile as an infimum
(the upper-bound direction); no profile lower bound enters.  Now, on
$\mathcal A_{\kappa,t}$ the defining inequality reads
$\kappa\,e_tW_{\rm cut}\le Q_t$, and dividing by $\kappa>0$ gives
$e_tW_{\rm cut}\le Q_t/\kappa$ there; off $\mathcal A_{\kappa,t}$ the left side of
\eqref{eq:sol-screen-domination} vanishes while $Q_t=s_t\norm{K_t}_\HS^2\ge0$.  This proves
\eqref{eq:sol-screen-domination} with no case excluded.

For the record, the degenerate state $K_t=0$ is covered by the conventions of
Lemma~\ref{lem:lyapunov-stein-duality} and charges nothing: there $Q_t=0$ and
$\lambda_{\rm cut}(A_t,0)=0$, so $W_{\rm cut}=1$ and membership in
$\mathcal A_{\kappa,t}$ reads $0\ge\kappa e_t$, which (as $e_t\ge0$) holds exactly when
$e_t=0$; in either case both sides of \eqref{eq:sol-screen-domination} vanish.

Integrating \eqref{eq:sol-screen-domination} in time and taking expectations,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sol-step1}
  \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}
    e_t\,W_{\rm cut}(A_t,K_t)\,\one_{\mathcal A_{\kappa,t}}\,\dd t
  \;\le\;\frac1\kappa\,\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}Q_t\,\dd t .
\end{equation}

\medskip
\emph{Step 2: tight-window conversion to Riccati currencies.}
On $\{t<\tau_\eta\}$ we have $\abs{p_t-1/2}\le\eta\le1/4$, hence
$s_t\ge1/4-\eta^2\ge3/16>1/8$, and the second inequality of the certified conversion
Lemma~\ref{lem:stein-vs-source} applies:
\[
  Q_t=\frac{\calS_{\mu_t}(E)}{s_t}\;\le\;2S_t+64\,\eta^2D_t
  \qquad\text{on }\{t<\tau_\eta\}.
\]
The single time $t=\tau_\eta$ has Lebesgue measure zero in the time integral, so
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sol-step2}
  \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}Q_t\,\dd t
  \;\le\;2\,\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}S_t\,\dd t
        +64\,\eta^2\,\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}D_t\,\dd t .
\end{equation}

\medskip
\emph{Step 3: the coordinate source budget under $0<p_0<1$.}
We prove
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sol-step3}
  \E\int_0^\infty S_t\,\dd t\;\le\;\sum_{i\in J}(R_0)_{ii}\;\le\;k .
\end{equation}
This is part~(i) of Theorem~\ref{thm:budget}, whose certified dossier
(\texttt{solutions/kls-product-covariance.tex}) records in its dependency audit that this part
uses only $0<p_0<1$ and not the packaged coarse balance window $p_0\in[2/5,3/5]$; for the
reviewer's convenience we reproduce the three-line certified argument, which cites only
unconditional inputs.  At every finite time, $(\mu_t,E)$ is a finite-second-moment product
pair with a $J$-measurable cut and $0<p_t<1$, so Lemma~\ref{lem:block} gives that $G_t$ is
supported on $J\times J$; hence its columns $G_te_i$ vanish for $i\notin J$ and
\[
  S_t=s_t\norm{G_t}_\HS^2=\sum_{i\in J}s_t\abs{G_te_i}^2 .
\]
For each fixed $i$, the unconditional per-direction estimate
Corollary~\ref{cor:per-direction} gives
$\E\int_0^Ts_t\abs{G_te_i}^2\,\dd t\le e_i^TR_0e_i=(R_0)_{ii}$ for every $T>0$.  Since
$R_0=A_0-B_0\preceq A_0=I_n$ by isotropy of the product, $(R_0)_{ii}\le1$.  Summing over
$i\in J$ and letting $T\to\infty$ by monotone convergence proves
\eqref{eq:sol-step3}.  In particular, since $S\ge0$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sol-step3b}
  \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}S_t\,\dd t\;\le\;k .
\end{equation}

\medskip
\emph{Step 4: the dissipation budget.}
We prove
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sol-step4}
  \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}D_t\,\dd t\;\le\;1+k .
\end{equation}
By Theorem~\ref{thm:scalar-riccati} there is a scalar local martingale $M$ with
$\dd r_t=\dd M_t+(S_t-D_t)\,\dd t$.  In the regular class (M2), let
$(\sigma_m)_{m\ge1}$ be an increasing sequence of bounded stopping times tending to infinity
such that each stopped $M^{\sigma_m}$ is a true martingale and all stopped coefficients are
integrable — exactly the localizing times used in the certified dossiers
\texttt{solutions/kls-localization-riccati-core.tex} and
\texttt{solutions/kls-product-covariance.tex}.  Optional stopping at the bounded stopping
time $T\wedge\tau_\eta\wedge\sigma_m$ gives
\[
  \E\, r_{T\wedge\tau_\eta\wedge\sigma_m}
  \;=\;r_0+\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta\wedge\sigma_m}(S_t-D_t)\,\dd t ,
\]
whence, discarding $\E\,r_{T\wedge\tau_\eta\wedge\sigma_m}\ge0$ and enlarging the
$S$-integral to $[0,\infty)$ (both integrands are nonnegative),
\[
  \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta\wedge\sigma_m}D_t\,\dd t
  \;\le\;r_0+\E\int_0^\infty S_t\,\dd t
  \;\le\;r_0+k
\]
by \eqref{eq:sol-step3}.  Since $D\ge0$ and
$T\wedge\tau_\eta\wedge\sigma_m\uparrow T\wedge\tau_\eta$, monotone convergence in $m$ gives
$\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}D_t\,\dd t\le r_0+k$.  Finally $r_0\le1$: the covariance
decomposition gives $B_0\preceq A_0=I_n$, and $B_0=s_0\delta_0\delta_0^T$ has rank at most
one, so its trace $r_0$ is its only nonzero eigenvalue and is at most~$1$.  This proves
\eqref{eq:sol-step4}.

\medskip
\emph{Step 5: combination.}
Chaining \eqref{eq:sol-step1}, \eqref{eq:sol-step2}, \eqref{eq:sol-step3b}, and
\eqref{eq:sol-step4},
\[
  \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}
    e_t\,W_{\rm cut}(A_t,K_t)\,\one_{\mathcal A_{\kappa,t}}\,\dd t
  \;\le\;\frac1\kappa\Bigl(2k+64\,\eta^2(1+k)\Bigr),
\]
which is \eqref{eq:sol-split-screened-supply}.  Every bound used —
\eqref{eq:sol-screen-domination}, the conversion constants $2$ and $64\eta^2$, the source
budget $k$, and the dissipation budget $1+k$ — is independent of $T$, of $n$, and of the
spectator factors, which never entered; the constant is therefore total-budget as claimed.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Shape of the bound; what is not claimed]
\label{rem:sol-shape}
The bound \eqref{eq:sol-split-screened-supply} is a \emph{total budget}: a single constant
$c_E(k,\eta,\kappa)=(2k+64\eta^2(1+k))/\kappa$ controlling the screened weighted excess over
the entire window $[0,T\wedge\tau_\eta]$ for \emph{every} $T>0$ simultaneously.  It is not of
the form $C(k)\,T$, and this dossier does not assert any fixed-time expected-source estimate
$\sup_{t\le T_0}\E[Q_t\one_{\mathcal A_{\kappa,t}}\one_{\{t<\tau_\eta\}}]\le C(k)$ that would
yield such a form; that upgrade is recorded as open in the probe record.  Likewise nothing
here bears on the trace-upgrade cluster
(\ref{q:upgrade}, high-rank \ref{q:stein-weighted}, \ref{q:alignment}) or on the general
(non-product) screened supply: no occupation estimate, equivalence, or comparison across
that cluster is proved or implied (constraint~6).
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Interpretation: the weight is cut-local on the split class]
\label{rem:sol-harmonic-mean}
This remark is interpretive and is used nowhere in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:sol-split-screened-supply}.  Pathwise, $A_t$ is diagonal
(Proposition~\ref{prop:products}) with positive diagonal entries $A_t^{(i)}$ in the regular
class, and $K_t=P_JK_tP_J$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:block}.  By the certified eigenbasis identity
\eqref{eq:sol-cut-harmonic-mean} of
\texttt{solutions/lem-lyapunov-stein-duality.tex} (Remark on the auxiliary harmonic-mean
formula), $\lambda_{\rm cut}(A_t,K_t)$ is the $\abs{(K_t)_{ij}}^2$-weighted harmonic mean of
$(A_t^{(i)}+A_t^{(j)})/2$ over $(i,j)\in J\times J$, hence
\[
  \lambda_{\rm cut}(A_t,K_t)\;\le\;\max_{i\in J}A_t^{(i)} :
\]
on the split class the weight sees only the $J$-block covariance scale; spectator covariance
spikes are invisible to both $Q_t$ and $W_{\rm cut}$, consistently with the uniformity in
$n$ and in the spectators of the theorem.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[General split laws: approximant convention and explicit gap]
\label{rem:sol-general-class}
For a general product $\mu=\bigotimes_i\mu^{(i)}$ of isotropic one-dimensional log-concave
laws and a general $J$-measurable cut with $0<p_0<1$ and $P_0(E)<\infty$, statement
\eqref{eq:sol-split-screened-supply} is \emph{defined on approximants}, per convention (M2)
and Section~5(5) of the probe record: apply Theorem~\ref{thm:sol-split-screened-supply} to
the coordinatewise product-preserving truncated and smoothed approximants used in the
certified scheme of \texttt{solutions/kls-product-covariance.tex} (each factor truncated,
smoothed, and renormalized to isotropic position — which preserves the class — and the cut
regularized within the $J$-coordinates, preserving $J$-measurability); the constant
$(2k+64\eta^2(1+k))/\kappa$ is uniform over the approximation.  What is \emph{not} proved
here, and is flagged as an explicit open technical step, is the limit interchange for the
screened integrand itself: the indicator $\one_{\mathcal A_{\kappa,t}}$ does not converge
monotonically along the approximation, so the left side of
\eqref{eq:sol-split-screened-supply} evaluated directly on the limiting rough data is not
obtained from the approximant bound by Fatou.  Any consumer of the general-class statement
must either work with the approximants or close this interchange separately.
\end{remark}

\subsection*{Auxiliary lemma: constant-supply extension of tight-window consumption}

The following lemma is \emph{not} part of Theorem~\ref{thm:sol-split-screened-supply} and is
not needed by its proof.  It extends the certified
Corollary~\ref{cor:tight-window-consumption} by allowing a constant additive term in the
absorptive Carleson input — exactly the shape a total-budget supply such as
\eqref{eq:sol-split-screened-supply} would feed, as recorded in Section~1 of the probe.  It
is stated in the general (not split-specific) localization setting of the certified Riccati
dossier, in the regular class of (M2) with its regularization-independent constants.

\begin{lemma}[tight-window consumption with a constant supply term]
\label{lem:sol-constant-supply-consumption}
Let $\mu$ be isotropic and log-concave, $E$ a cut, and fix $0<\eta\le1/6$ with
$\abs{p_0-1/2}\le\eta/2$.  Suppose that for constants $c,C_0,C_1\ge0$, a number
$\alpha<1$, a horizon $T_0>0$, and every $T\le T_0$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sol-constant-carleson}
  \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}S_t\,\dd t
  \;\le\;c+C_0T
   +C_1\,\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}r_t\,\dd t
   +\alpha\,\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}D_t\,\dd t ,
\end{equation}
and that
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:sol-apriori-finite}
  \E\int_0^{T_0\wedge\tau_\eta}D_t\,\dd t<\infty
\end{equation}
(automatic in the regular class (M2)).  Set
$C_*=(1+c+C_0T_0)\,e^{C_1T_0}$.  Then for every positive
$T_*\le\min\bigl(T_0,\ 4\eta^2/(9C_*)\bigr)$,
\[
  \Prob\bigl\{\min(p_{T_*},q_{T_*})\ge1/3\bigr\}\;\ge\;\frac12 ,
\]
and hence Lemma~\ref{lem:survival-implies-kls} gives
$\mu^+(E)\ge c'\sqrt{T_*}$ for a numerical $c'>0$, with $T_*$ depending only on
$(T_0,c,C_0,C_1,\eta)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
This is the proof of the certified Corollary~\ref{cor:tight-window-consumption}
(Section~4 of \texttt{solutions/kls-localization-}\allowbreak\texttt{riccati-core.tex}) with
one line changed in the Gronwall input; we run it in full.  Let $u(T)=\E\,r_{T\wedge\tau_\eta}$ for
$0\le T\le T_0$.  In the regular class, $r_t=\Tr B_t\le\Tr A_t$ is bounded on bounded
intervals by the squared support diameter, so $u$ is finite and bounded on $[0,T_0]$, and it
is measurable ($r$ has continuous paths, so $u$ is a pointwise limit of continuous
functions by dominated convergence); the constants produced below do not depend on the
regularization.

Integrate Theorem~\ref{thm:scalar-riccati} at the bounded stopping times
$T\wedge\tau_\eta\wedge\sigma_m$, with $(\sigma_m)$ the localizing sequence of (M2), and let
$m\uparrow\infty$: Fatou on the left (as $r\ge0$ has continuous paths) and monotone
convergence for the two nonnegative occupation terms give
\[
  u(T)\;\le\;r_0+\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}S_t\,\dd t
             -\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}D_t\,\dd t .
\]
Insert \eqref{eq:sol-constant-carleson} and use $r_0\le1$ (rank-one $B_0\preceq A_0=I$).
The term $(\alpha-1)\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}D_t\,\dd t$ is nonpositive and finite by
\eqref{eq:sol-apriori-finite} with $\alpha<1$, so it may be discarded — this is the one
place the absorption margin and the a priori finiteness are used — leaving
\[
  u(T)\;\le\;1+c+C_0T
        +C_1\,\E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}r_t\,\dd t
  \;\le\;1+c+C_0T+C_1\int_0^Tu(t)\,\dd t ,
\]
the last inequality by the pathwise bound
$\one_{\{t<\tau_\eta\}}r_t\le r_{t\wedge\tau_\eta}$ and Tonelli.  Gronwall's inequality for
the bounded measurable $u$ yields
\[
  u(T)\;\le\;(1+c+C_0T)\,e^{C_1T}\;\le\;C_*
  \qquad(0\le T\le T_0).
\]
Before exit, $\abs{p_t-1/2}\le\eta\le1/6$ gives
$s_t\ge1/4-\eta^2\ge2/9$, hence
$\abs{\delta_t}^2=r_t/s_t\le\tfrac92 r_t$ and
\[
  \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau_\eta}\abs{\delta_t}^2\,\dd t
  \;\le\;\frac92\int_0^T\E\bigl[\one_{\{t<\tau_\eta\}}r_t\bigr]\,\dd t
  \;\le\;\frac92\,C_*T .
\]
Since $\abs{p_0-1/2}\le\eta/2$, continuity of $p$ forces
$\sup_{t\le T\wedge\tau_\eta}\abs{p_t-p_0}\ge\eta/2$ on $\{\tau_\eta\le T\}$.  Doob's $L^2$
inequality applied to the stopped mass martingale, whose quadratic variation is
$\dd[p]_t=s_t^2\abs{\delta_t}^2\,\dd t$ with $s_t\le1/4$, gives
\[
  \Prob(\tau_\eta\le T)
  \;\le\;\frac4{\eta^2}\,\E[p]_{T\wedge\tau_\eta}
  \;\le\;\frac{4}{16\,\eta^2}\cdot\frac92\,C_*T
  \;=\;\frac{9C_*T}{8\eta^2}.
\]
For $T_*\le\min(T_0,4\eta^2/(9C_*))$ this is at most $1/2$, and on
$\{\tau_\eta>T_*\}$ we have $\min(p_{T_*},q_{T_*})\ge1/2-\eta\ge1/3$.  The boundary
consequence is Lemma~\ref{lem:survival-implies-kls} with
$(T_0,c_0,b_0)=(T_*,1/2,1/3)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Role of the lemma; no companion claimed]
\label{rem:sol-no-companion}
Lemma~\ref{lem:sol-constant-supply-consumption} shows the consumption chain does not
intrinsically require an $O(T)$ supply: a $T$-uniform constant such as the right-hand side of
\eqref{eq:sol-split-screened-supply} is an admissible input shape, at the price of a shorter
survival time $T_*\asymp\eta^2/C_*$.  This dossier proves \emph{only} the supply side on the
split class.  No screened trace companion (the estimate (C)/(22) of the probe record) is
proved or assumed here, and no KLS-type conclusion is drawn from
Theorem~\ref{thm:sol-split-screened-supply}.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{Obstructions respected.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{obs:two-tail} (Proposition~\ref{prop:two-tail}): that fence kills slice-wise
  absolute-scale Stein estimates and calibrates the necessary covariance-weight power $5/2$.
  Theorem~\ref{thm:sol-split-screened-supply} is a time-integrated expectation bound, not a
  slice-wise estimate; it retains exactly the calibrated weight
  $(1+\lambda_{\rm cut})^{5/2}$, whose two-tail value is
  $\lambda_{\rm cut}(A_\Lambda,K_\Lambda)=\Lambda$ by the certified calibration
  \eqref{eq:sol-cut-two-tail}, so no weaker power is smuggled in; and the two-tail initial
  laws $N(0,\diag(\Lambda,1,\dots,1))$, $\Lambda>1$, lie outside the hypothesis class (their
  first factor is not isotropic).  States of two-tail type reached dynamically remain
  chargeable by the screen and are paid for through the budget $Q_t/\kappa$; the theorem
  never discards them.
  \item \texttt{obs:circularity}: no lower bound on the moving profile
  $I_{\mu_t}(p_t)$ — and no profile information beyond its definition as an infimum, used
  once in Step~1 in the upper-bound direction $I_{\mu_t}(p_t)\le P_t(E)$ to record
  $e_t\ge0$ — enters any step.  The excess-identity refinement fenced by this obstruction is
  not used; indeed $e_t$ is touched only through the pathwise screen inequality
  \eqref{eq:sol-screen-domination}.
\end{itemize}
