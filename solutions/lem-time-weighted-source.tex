\section*{Solution candidate: scale-weighted all-cut source budget}
\label{sec:sol-time-weighted-source}

\paragraph{Scope and notation.}
Let $\mu$ be isotropic and log-concave on $\R^n$, and let $E$ be a fixed measurable set with
$0<\mu(E)<1$.  Along Eldan localization write
\[
 p_t=\mu_t(E),\quad q_t=1-p_t,\quad s_t=p_tq_t,
 \quad \delta_t=m_t^E-m_t^{E^c},
\]
\[
 G_t=\Sigma_t^E-\Sigma_t^{E^c},\qquad
 B_t=s_t\delta_t\delta_t^T,\qquad
 r_t=\Tr B_t=s_t\abs{\delta_t}^2,
\]
\[
 S_t=s_t\norm{G_t}_{\HS}^2,qquad
 D_t=2s_t\delta_t^TA_t\delta_t-r_t^2,
 \quad A_t=\Cov(\mu_t).
\]
The finite-time localization likelihood is strictly positive relative to $\mu$, so
$p_t,q_t>0$ at every finite time.  No balance assumption is imposed on the cut.

\begin{theorem}[refined form of Lemma~\ref{lem:time-weighted-source}]
\label{thm:sol-time-weighted-source}
For every $T>0$,
\begin{equation}
 \E\int_0^T t^2\bigl(S_t+r_t^2\bigr)\dd t
 \le T^2\E r_T\le T.
 \label{eq:sol-time-weighted-source}
\end{equation}
Consequently, if $\tau$ is any stopping time (in particular, the exit time from any balanced
window), then, solely by positivity,
\begin{equation}
 \E\int_0^{T\wedge\tau}t^2\bigl(S_t+r_t^2\bigr)\dd t
 \le \E\int_0^Tt^2\bigl(S_t+r_t^2\bigr)\dd t
 \le T^2\E r_T\le T.
 \label{eq:sol-time-weighted-stopped}
\end{equation}
The same conclusion holds if the integrand is further restricted to a deterministic time
interval.  In particular, this is not an unweighted source estimate at $t=0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The conditional covariance decomposition is
\begin{equation}
 A_t=p_t\Sigma_t^E+q_t\Sigma_t^{E^c}+B_t.
 \label{eq:sol-time-weighted-decomposition}
\end{equation}
Thus $0\preceq B_t\preceq A_t$.  The matrix $B_t$ has rank at most one, and its only possible
nonzero eigenvalue is its trace $r_t$.  For $t>0$, the posterior is $t$-uniformly log-concave,
so the Brascamp--Lieb covariance cap gives
\begin{equation}
 0\le r_t=\lambda_{\max}(B_t)\le\lambda_{\max}(A_t)\le\frac1t.
 \label{eq:sol-time-weighted-r-cap}
\end{equation}
Moreover, using $A_t\preceq t^{-1}I$ and $B_t\succeq0$,
\[
 s_t\delta_t^TA_t\delta_t=\Tr(A_tB_t)
 \le\frac1t\Tr B_t=\frac{r_t}{t}.
\]
It follows that the damping has the pathwise upper bound
\begin{equation}
 D_t\le\frac{2r_t}{t}-r_t^2,
 \qquad t>0.
 \label{eq:sol-time-weighted-D-cap}
\end{equation}

By the scalar Riccati identity, Theorem~\ref{thm:scalar-riccati}, there is a continuous local
martingale $M$ such that
\[
 \dd r_t=\dd M_t+(S_t-D_t)\dd t.
\]
Multiplication by the deterministic function $t^2$ and
\eqref{eq:sol-time-weighted-D-cap} give, for $t>0$,
\begin{align}
 \dd(t^2r_t)
 &=t^2\dd M_t+
   \bigl(2tr_t+t^2S_t-t^2D_t\bigr)\dd t \notag\\
 &\ge t^2\dd M_t+t^2\bigl(S_t+r_t^2\bigr)\dd t.
 \label{eq:sol-time-weighted-product}
\end{align}

We now justify expectation and the endpoint rather than taking an expectation of the local
martingale formally.  Fix $0<\varepsilon<T$, and localize the weighted local martingale
$L_t=\int_\varepsilon^t u^2\dd M_u$.  Choose increasing stopping times
$\rho_k\ge\varepsilon$, with $\rho_k\uparrow\infty$ almost surely, such that the stopped
increments of $L$ are true martingales on $[\varepsilon,T]$, and put
$\theta_k=T\wedge\rho_k$.  Integrating \eqref{eq:sol-time-weighted-product} to $\theta_k$ and
taking expectations yields
\begin{equation}
 \E\int_\varepsilon^{\theta_k}
      t^2\bigl(S_t+r_t^2\bigr)\dd t
 \le \E\bigl[\theta_k^2r_{\theta_k}\bigr]
      -\varepsilon^2\E r_\varepsilon.
 \label{eq:sol-time-weighted-localized}
\end{equation}
The cap \eqref{eq:sol-time-weighted-r-cap}, applied at $\theta_k\ge\varepsilon$, gives
\[
 0\le\theta_k^2r_{\theta_k}\le\theta_k\le T.
\]
Since $\theta_k\uparrow T$, dominated convergence applies to this terminal term, while monotone
convergence applies to the nonnegative occupation integral.  Hence
\begin{equation}
 \E\int_\varepsilon^Tt^2\bigl(S_t+r_t^2\bigr)\dd t
 \le T^2\E r_T-\varepsilon^2\E r_\varepsilon.
 \label{eq:sol-time-weighted-epsilon}
\end{equation}
There is no hidden use of the Brascamp--Lieb cap at $t=0$: isotropy and
\eqref{eq:sol-time-weighted-decomposition} give $B_0\preceq A_0=I$ and $r_0\le1$, while for
$\varepsilon>0$ the cap gives
$0\le\varepsilon^2\E r_\varepsilon\le\varepsilon$.  Letting
$\varepsilon\downarrow0$ in \eqref{eq:sol-time-weighted-epsilon}, again by monotone
convergence on the left, proves the first inequality in
\eqref{eq:sol-time-weighted-source}.  Finally
$0\le T^2r_T\le T$ pathwise by \eqref{eq:sol-time-weighted-r-cap}, proving the second one.
Equation~\eqref{eq:sol-time-weighted-stopped} then follows only from
$S_t+r_t^2\ge0$.

For completeness, the preceding calculation is stable under the repository's regularization
convention.  At the regular level one truncates and smooths the log-concave density and the cut,
then puts the measure back in isotropic position; bounded stopping makes every stochastic
integral above a true martingale.  The scalar Riccati identity is already certified for the
general law by precisely this passage.  On each strip $[\varepsilon,T]$, its approximation
gives convergence of the posterior masses and first and second cut moments (after passage to a
subsequence if necessary), hence of $r_t$ and $S_t$ for almost every $(\omega,t)$.  Fatou's
lemma applies to the nonnegative occupation term.  At the terminal time the uniform pathwise
bound $0\le T^2r_T\le T$ gives uniform integrability, so the terminal expectations pass to the
limit.  The constants are independent of the regularization.  Sending
$\varepsilon\downarrow0$ as above completes the passage for an arbitrary isotropic
log-concave law and arbitrary measurable nontrivial cut.  Thus no smoothness or compact-support
hypothesis remains in the theorem.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Obstructions respected.}
The ledger gives \texttt{lem:time-weighted-source} no \texttt{bounded\_by} edge.  The relevant
Eldan-route fences are nevertheless respected.  No radial or projection-only family is used to
infer a tensor trace bound, so \texttt{obs:proj-ceiling} is not crossed.  The estimate permits
source of order $t^{-2}$ and retains the factor $t^2$, so it makes no forbidden slice-wise
absolute-scale assertion in the two-tail regime of \texttt{obs:two-tail}.  It assumes no
relative-scale covariance occupation estimate of the kind fenced by
\texttt{obs:relative-ceiling}.  It also uses neither the crude $\Xi_T$ estimate, a localized
isoperimetric profile, nor a product-cut counterexample, so
\texttt{obs:crude-insufficient}, \texttt{obs:circularity}, and
\texttt{obs:rank-one-refuted} are untouched.

\paragraph{Status and exclusions.}
The only ledger dependency is the already certified Theorem~\ref{thm:scalar-riccati}; its own
dependency \texttt{lem:matrix-riccati} is certified as well.  The other analytic input is the
standard posterior Brascamp--Lieb covariance cap.  There is no unresolved hypothesis, so the
stated lemma is unconditional.  This dossier does not remove the quadratic time weight, does
not prove \texttt{q:upgrade} or an all-cut Carleson estimate, makes no claim about
\texttt{q:stein-weighted} or \texttt{q:alignment}, and proves no KLS conclusion.
