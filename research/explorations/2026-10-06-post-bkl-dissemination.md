# Dissemination after BKL: contributions and limits of novelty claims

## Question examined

What remains worth presenting after the BKL announcement, and what may honestly
be called a contribution of this manuscript? This is an editorial and preliminary
bibliographic assessment, not a proof review or a priority determination.

## What we learned

*Judgment.* Three kinds of contribution must be separated: a reconstruction of
published or announced mathematics, specific results developed in this project,
and a record of assisted research with its corrections and failures. BKL's proof
is attributed to Bizeul, Klartag and Lehec. Identifying its endpoint in earlier
work here does not confer priority over the mechanism establishing it.

*Observed in the existing proof.* `thm:cmh-1d` identifies the project's CMH
quantity with an ordinary Poincaré ratio through adjoint-operator duality.
Its consequence with constant four is explicitly attributed to known
one-dimensional Poincaré bounds. The identity and its domain treatment are
useful exposition and a reusable local result; certification does not establish
that this formulation is new. Related primary literature includes Germain and
Swan, [arXiv:2112.02638v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02638v3), whose abstract
describes weighted Poincaré constants via Stein integration and inverse
Sturm–Liouville operators. This is a comparison lead, not an assertion that
their theorem equals the CMH identity.

*Observed in the existing proof.* `lem:fiber-polynomial-floor` gives a concrete
restriction on proposed simplex counterexamples: a fixed polynomial degree
cannot produce an asymptotically vanishing all-frame certificate. Its proof
combines an interval polynomial inverse inequality, the isotropic frame
identity and the known simplex Poincaré bound. This remains useful for choosing
tests independently of the full KLS question. A preliminary search also locates
Kook and Vempala, [arXiv:2608.16878v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16878v1), on
spectral gaps of Hit-and-Run. Its stated problem concerns Markov-chain gaps,
not the exact fixed-degree all-frame assertion here; no implication or novelty
conclusion is inferred without comparing normalizations and quantifiers.

*Decision.* Public prose will say these results are proved or reconstructed
here, with the attribution of their inputs. It will not describe them as new
to the literature on the basis of this preliminary search. A standalone
original-research submission would require a fuller theorem-level comparison.

*Judgment.* Alternative proofs deserve effort when they offer a concrete gain:
a simpler argument, deterministic mechanism, constants, broader hypotheses,
or information about extremal cases. Their continued viability is separate
from the allocation of effort. No route is closed by this assessment.

## What resists

The preliminary bibliography does not establish first occurrence of either
result. Nor does the existence of a ledger and independent agent checks measure
the effectiveness of this research process. Any methodological account must
include the actual corrected errors, unresolved attempts and missed mechanism,
not just the final set of certificates.

## Proposed next step

Present the integrated exposition with source attribution and exact local
verification statuses. Keep mathematical originality claims out of the public
description until a separate theorem-level literature comparison supports
them. Publication remains a separate human reading and deployment step.
