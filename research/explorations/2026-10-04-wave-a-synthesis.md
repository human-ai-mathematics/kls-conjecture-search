### CMH: admissibility precedes differentiation

*Established analytically in a checkpoint, not independently certified as a node.*
[The perturbation analysis](2026-10-04-wave-a-cmh-variation.md) shows that
two-sided target-linear perturbations on the fixed exponential-product support
are affine tilts. Transporting the full quotient gives zero second variation at
every polynomial degree, including one through four. The corresponding
exponential–Gaussian target-linear family does not provide a coupling either.
These conclusions do not exclude nonlinear paths, moving supports, or the
canonical source-linear ansatz of `conj:cmh-second-variation`.

`ap:c-solenoidal-perturbation` stays active, with the explicit nonlinear boundary
path $\beta=2+\varepsilon^2$ as its next test. Transfer to regular compact-target
approximants remains a separate obligation. A positive finite-degree coefficient
still needs an admissible parameter and remainder estimate overcoming the deficit
below four. No CMH conjecture is refuted or certified by this mission.

### Product-simplex cones: a certified sharp linear gate

*Established and independently certified.*
`prop:product-simplex-cone-gate` gives the full spectrum of the normalized gate
matrix for every product of positive-dimensional simplices and every real
$\beta\ge n$. Its transverse gaps reduce to a polynomial with nonnegative
coefficients. The bound is two; equality at $\beta=n$ holds in all directions for
a single simplex base and only in the axis for multiple factors. Intervals count
as one-dimensional simplices.

[Research handoff](2026-10-04-wave-a-cone-gate.md),
[proof](../../solutions/prop-product-simplex-cone-gate.md), and
[independent pass](../reviews/2026-10-04-wave-a-cone-gate-review.md).

This promotes the geometric content of `cand:cone-transverse-equality-simplex`
with its interval parametrization corrected, and closes that candidate here.
Its literal condition omitted the single interval representation when no
higher-dimensional simplex factor was listed; this is not a new geometric
equality phenomenon. `ap:c-gate-zero` remains active for general bases and the
universal gate. A linear-sector result does not establish nonlinear CMH.

### Fibers: every fixed polynomial degree has a positive floor

*Established and independently certified.* `lem:fiber-polynomial-floor` proves,
for every isotropic uniform simplex, every admissible frame, and every polynomial
of degree at most $k\ge1$,

$$
\mathcal D_{\mu_m,\rho}(f)
\ge\frac{12}{k(k+1)^2(k+2)}\int|\nabla f|^2\,d\mu_m
\ge\frac{3}{k(k+1)^2(k+2)}\operatorname{Var}_{\mu_m}(f).
$$

Consequently $\Lambda_{m,3}\ge1/80$ for every $m\ge2$, and no fixed-degree
all-frame upper certificate can tend to zero with dimension. The proof uses a
reverse polynomial inequality on each uniform chord, frame isotropy, and the
already certified Dirichlet Poincaré bound. It uses no numerical experiment.

[Research handoff](2026-10-04-wave-a-fiber-dual.md),
[proof](../../solutions/lem-fiber-polynomial-floor.md), and
[independent pass](../reviews/2026-10-04-wave-a-fiber-floor-review.md).

The exact values for $m=3,\ldots,8$ remain unresolved. The preregistered finite-range
criterion was not evaluated, rather than failed. The proof does not determine it:
a floor of $1/80$ is compatible with a final value below $0.1$. The symmetry
reduction in the research checkpoint is outside the certified dossier's scope.
`ap:f-simplex-dual` stays active with an explicitly growing-degree/nonpolynomial
objective. The full $L^2$ frame conjecture and `ap:f-frame-construction` remain
open; the degree-dependent bound is compatible with the root-cap obstruction.

### Survival bridge: a real repair, then a fresh pass

*Established by independent review.*
[The first review](../reviews/2026-10-04-wave-a-survival-independent.md) returned
`revise`: the shared dossier's reduced-boundary argument did not justify its
outer-Minkowski assertion for arbitrary measurable cuts, and the nonsmooth

counterexample to `lem:survival-implies-kls` was found.

A researcher supplied a
[standalone replacement](../../solutions/lem-survival-implies-kls.md), with
[repair handoff](2026-10-04-wave-a-survival-repair.md). It uses neighborhood
increments, bounded indicator-mass martingales and deterministic-sequence Fatou
for the actual Minkowski content. Curvature-preserving approximation is applied
to each posterior, without approximating the survival event. A new independent
[review passed](../reviews/2026-10-04-wave-a-survival-repair-review.md), checking all
four requested repairs and the actual published sources.

The ledger replaces only the survival lemma's historical proof record by this
certified standalone proof. The other claims using the shared core keep their
existing records, and the old dossier and reports are preserved. The canonical
survival statement is unchanged; its universal event premise is not discharged.
