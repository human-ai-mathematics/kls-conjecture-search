# KLS conditional-fiber route admission

Date: 2026-08-27

Role: orchestrator

## Decision

Admit `conditional-fiber-frame` as a fourth live KLS route after the bounded route probe and
literature audit. The route is sufficiently distinct from Eldan localization, the fixed
eigenfunction route, and CMH: it seeks one measure-dependent but test-independent isotropic frame
of conditional line resamplings whose inverse-conditional-variance normalized form has a
universal $L^2$ gap.

Admission changes no conjecture status. The structural bridge, universal frame question, and
repository vertex-cap refuter enter as `open`. Sasada's published negative-rate exchange result
enters as an imported proposition, not as a global obstruction node.

## Exact logical shape

For an admissible even frame $\rho$ with

$$
d\int\theta\theta^T\,d\rho(\theta)=I_d,
$$

the candidate form is

$$
\mathcal D_{\mu,\rho}(f)
=d\int\!\int
\frac{\operatorname{Var}_{\mu_{\theta,z}}(f(z+T\theta))}
{\operatorname{Var}_{\mu_{\theta,z}}(T)}
\,d\bar\mu_\theta(z)d\rho(\theta).
$$

The staged structural lemma asks for the closed reversible form, its form-sense generator,
calibrations, and the factor-$4$ comparison
$\mathcal D_{\mu,\rho}(f)\le4\int|\nabla f|^2d\mu$. The route gate then asks for one $\rho_\mu$
fixed before all test functions and a universal lower form gap.

## Fence preserved

The natural $A_{m-1}$ root frame on the uniform simplex has gap $O(m^{-2})$, both by the
repository's exact vertex-cap calculation and by published negative-rate exchange prior art.
This kills that implementation only. It does not imply that every admissible frame fails:
permutation-invariant frames may mix continuously many direction orbits. The decisive negative
object is an all-frame simplex dual certificate, not another root-orbit calculation or a finite
floating sweep.
