# BKL certification and the continuing research programme

## Question examined

What does the reconstructed BKL v1 proof establish, and which research objectives
remain after `conj:kls` is proved? This completes `ap:bkl-reconstruction` and
updates the initial decisions in the 2026-10-06 BKL integration checkpoint.

**Established.** The stronger `cor:bkl-uniform-conditional-initialization`

a proof independent of BKL and KLS, together with the still-needed comparison.

Thus there remain ten active research routes and six blocked routes. Keeping
priority 1 is a choice to investigate a different mechanism near the already
certified polynomial machinery, not a claim that its completion is likely.
Priority 2 targets concrete discriminating structural tests; priority 3 remains
available without starting all ten investigations at once.

**Established framework change.** A proved target permits continued alternative
proofs and stronger statements. A refuted target still forbids active proof
search. Dependencies remain node-level: neither a second dossier nor an acyclic
graph by itself certifies independence. Dossiers and reviews must identify inputs.

## What resists

No independent alternative KLS proof, sharp CMH estimate, all-frame fiber bound,
or universal occupation estimate is established by this integration. The
literary value of a checked reconstruction is separate from novelty of local
results; the preliminary assessment in the post-BKL dissemination checkpoint
makes no bibliographic novelty claim. There is no new impossibility warranting
closure of an active research route.

## Proposed next step

Use the portfolio's explicit tests for subsequent research. Present the project
as an exposition of checked proofs, structural questions and alternative methods.
The initial search identified the coefficient endpoint but missed BKL's cumulant
and suspension mechanism; this limitation must accompany any methodological
account. A future methodological paper also needs a fuller audit of failures and
corrections and cannot infer effectiveness from the existence of this framework.
Publication remains a separate human decision after reading the manuscript.
