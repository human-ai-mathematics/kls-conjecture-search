# Song–Zhang proof reconstruction and reusable consequences

## Question examined

Can the source-proof audit `ap:polynomial-curvature-audit` deliver complete
proofs and reusable mathematical tools, rather than an announcement-level
integration of `thm:song-zhang-kls`?

## What we learned

*Established and independently certified.* Five complete dossiers reconstruct
the pinned arXiv:2610.01447v1 proof and its consequences. The eight canonical
nodes are `lem:sz-analytic-foundations`, `thm:sz-polynomial-variance`,
`thm:sz-curvature-comparison`, `thm:sz-iterated-curvature`,
`thm:sz-curvature-transfer`, `thm:song-zhang-kls`,
`cor:sz-affine-poincare`, and `prop:sz-exponential-coefficients-equivalence`.

fingerprints of its dossier and all required statements. The five reports are

`2026-10-03-sz-iteration-review.md`, `2026-10-03-sz-main-review.md`, and
`2026-10-03-sz-exponential-equivalence-review.md` under `research/reviews/`.
These are agent certifications under the repository contract, not human
acceptance or refereed publication of the source.

The source proof was checked at the level of its analytic domains, stochastic
cross variations, tensor normalization, symmetry-recovery constants,
overlapping error sums, degree and depth inductions, admissibility thresholds,
covariance exits, variance survival and scalar approximation. Reviews record
their individual checks and classical inputs. The proof neither substitutes
numerical tests for estimates nor treats the source's assertion as a premise.

*Established — reusable interfaces.* The generic curvature transfer isolates
the deterministic profile evaluation needed by Gaussian localization. The
exponential-coefficient equivalence identifies the exact uniform growth
condition that would yield KLS from the polynomial comparison. These are
proved consequences of the reconstructed mechanism; no historical novelty
claim is made. The latter is an equivalent-strength characterization, not a
new independent sufficient-condition route and not a proof of its premise.

*Observed — integration checks.* Chapter `modules/32-polynomial-curvature.md`

theorem retains its home in `modules/00-overview.md`. The overview, frontier,
synthesis, reading path and full-proof introduction now explain this chain.
Statement snapshots before and after both writer passes are identical.
Independent bounded synchronization found agreement between all eight nodes,
five dossiers, canonical statements and dependency edges. Full checks passed
before the certification transitions and after the final certifications and
brief/portfolio update. The final full check reports no draft dossier or
lifted certification; `git diff --check` is also clean.

## What resists

`conj:kls` remains open. The all-depth conclusion still incurs its depth
factor. A bounded-product replacement must also pay the growing initialization
and curvature-comparison thresholds; merely removing the leading multiplier
does not do so. The coefficient equivalence requires one constant uniform in
degree, dimension and regular measure, not a separate bound at each degree.
No CMH, adaptive trace, conditional-fiber or universal-time occupation
antecedent has been discharged. The existing four approaches retain their
targets and obstacles.

## Proposed next step

Close `ap:polynomial-curvature-audit`: its stated reconstruction and
certification objective is complete. Do not repeat it as an open source audit.
Any proposed improvement through this mechanism should first identify a
strictly stronger coefficient estimate or curvature profile and prove that its
constants meet every degree/depth threshold. The certified transfer can then
propagate that improvement without rebuilding the localization argument.
Use the exponential equivalence to test the strength and quantifiers of such
a proposal before calling it progress toward `conj:kls`.
