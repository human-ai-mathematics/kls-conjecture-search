# What the Song–Zhang revision changes in the earlier contract

## Question examined

Compare the new reconstruction with the finite-depth contract recorded in
`2026-10-04-wave-a-sz-contract.md`, particularly the continuing objectives
`ap:sz-conditional-initialization` and `ap:sz-recovery-probe`.

## What we learned

**Established interface distinction.** The old contract asked for a uniform
conditional startup and a near-unit replacement of the polynomial–curvature
comparison on the profiles used in its depth loop. Its summable-product
calculation was conditional on both estimates. It did not prove either.

The new common radius, [](#def:sz-v2-common-radius), changes the quantity
being iterated. The checked [](#prop:sz-v2-common-radius) converts its bound
to Poincare with a fixed numerical factor. In the proposed final composition
[](#thm:sz-v2-kls), this factor is paid once, after all refinements. The new
argument therefore does not need to replace the v1 comparison theorem's
fixed factor by a near-unit factor at every repetition.

**Established interface distinction.** The checked static transfer
[](#prop:sz-v2-static-coefficient-transfer) retains a uniform regular-law
coefficient premise and concludes a bound with exponent $d-1$. This is a
different interface from the Wave A startup with exponent $d$ and the
denominator $(d+1)^2$. A correspondence must account for those factors and
the moving degree range; similarity of the iteration does not identify the
two estimates. The already certified BKL initialization corollary remains
available as a consequence of BKL, not as an input to this separate proof.

**Established scope of the historical obstruction.** The Wave A obstruction
concerned the retained positive lag-kernel majorant and the retained
degree/depth thresholds. New joint-frame, orbit-window and normalization
estimates do not contradict it. No existing refutation or closed audit is
reopened merely because a different certificate succeeds.

**Methodological assessment.** The earlier search identified a coefficient
endpoint equivalent in strength to KLS and distinguished algebraic closure
from missing estimates. It did not supply the cumulant/suspension mechanism
or the new common-radius block construction. The new papers provide a
retrospective test of that diagnosis, not a priority claim or evidence that
the template would have discovered either proof. Bibliographic discovery
and local checking remain distinct from original research.

## What resists

No literal identification of the old startup and near-unit comparison
specifications with the source's replacement estimates follows from these
observations. No sharp CMH inequality, universal occupation estimate,
adaptive trace estimate or all-frame fiber construction follows by reversing
a sufficient condition for KLS. This checkpoint does not certify the full
outer refinement, whose status remains attached to its own statements.

## Proposed next step

Finish the independent checks of the source chain. Preserve the existing
states of the two SZ research routes while sharpening their next tests to
compare exact interfaces. Completion of the import route and completion of
either historical research objective are separate decisions. Keep the
remaining structural research routes and their recorded obstructions.
