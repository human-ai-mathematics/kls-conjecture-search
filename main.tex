\begin{abstract}
  This document maps the Kannan--Lov\'asz--Simonovits frontier --- the dimension-free Poincar\'e
  bound $C_{\mathrm P}\le K\lambda_{\max}(\mathrm{Cov})$ for an arbitrary isotropic log-concave
  measure, open since 1995. It distinguishes the sharp July 2026 radial and homogeneous-quadratic
  results from full KLS, develops the conditional fixed-cut Eldan program in detail, and records
  the separate fixed-eigenfunction, deterministic moment-map/Haar, and inverse-variance
  conditional-fiber routes with their exact open bottlenecks. It opens with an orientation to the
  conjecture, the August 2026 frontier, and the covariance-spike obstruction that constrains every
  route, followed by a self-contained survey of the five strategy families in the literature. It
  then develops this repository's own work: the shared localization machinery, the conditional
  fixed-cut Eldan program, the fixed-eigenfunction spectral route, and the distinct deterministic
  moment-map/Haar route with its commutator bottleneck --- that last route carrying a second,
  logically prior layer in which the proposed endpoint is given precise operator data, proved to
  dominate the affine Poincar\'e constant, and computed exactly on the line, on products, and on
  every log-concave Dirichlet law, together with the two reasons that endpoint may nonetheless be
  false. It closes by mapping each remaining gap in the literature onto an open node of this
  repository's KLS ledger.
\end{abstract}
