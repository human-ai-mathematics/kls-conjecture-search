\section{The two-color Stein dictionary and the operator-to-trace gap}
\label{sec:stein-dictionary}

Both Eldan subroutes consume the same covariance contrast of a cut, in two
normalizations. This short core section records the exact algebraic dictionary relating that
contrast to the Riccati source of Section~\ref{sec:riccati}, and isolates --- once --- the
exact Eldan--A gap and the covariance contrast that the proposed geometric route also seeks to
control. The bridge from Jacobi/Reilly boundary modes to this trace is not yet proved.

\subsection{The operator-to-trace gap}
\label{subsec:one-gap}

The per-direction Carleson estimate (Corollary~\ref{cor:per-direction}) is the unconditional
budget on the two-color source: with the occupation operator
\[
  \calM:=\E\int_0^\infty s_tG_t^2\dd t,
\]
estimate \eqref{eq:loewner-carleson} reads $\calM\preceq R_0\preceq I_n$, a dimension-free
bound at the level of quadratic forms. The Eldan--A KLS-strength statement is the \emph{trace}
$\Tr(\calM)$, whose only a priori bound is $\Tr R_0\le n$. The entire difficulty of the
all-cut route is this operator-to-trace upgrade. In the product model,
Question~\ref{q:alignment} isolates its incident-high residue; it is not an equivalent
reformulation of the full trace target. The weighted Stein-trace estimate of Eldan--B
(Question~\ref{q:stein-weighted}) faces analogous high-rank boundary modes, but the claimed
identification with this occupation operator awaits the almost-stability trace bridge of
Remark~\ref{rem:almost-stability-gap}. The available product argument does not prove a static
effective-rank bound: summing its coordinate budgets loses a factor $n$, whereas the
dimension-dependent early-window estimate follows independently from covariance moments.
This motivates an occupation-density bound forcing only $O(1)$ directions to be simultaneously
active on the early balanced window; it does not yet prove such temporal sparsity. This is the content of
Remark~\ref{rem:trace-upgrade-unification}. The dictionary below is the change of variables
tying the exact Riccati and Stein formulations together and supplying the common covariance currency
for the proposed geometric route.

\subsection{The two-color Stein representation}

\begin{proposition}[Two-color Stein representation]
\label{prop:stein-rep}
Let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $\R^n$ with finite fourth moment, mean $a$, covariance $A$,
$E$ measurable, $p=\nu(E)\in(0,1)$, and let $\delta,G,K=G+(q-p)\delta\delta^T$ be the two-color
quantities of Section~\ref{sec:notation}. For a symmetric matrix $M$ set
\[
  f_M(x)=(x-a)^TM(x-a)-\Tr(MA).
\]
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:stein-rep}
  \ell_{\nu,E}(M):=\int_E f_M\dd\nu
  =\Cov_\nu\bigl(\one_E,\;(x-a)^TM(x-a)\bigr)
  \;=\;s\bigl(\inner GM+(q-p)\,\delta^TM\delta\bigr)
  \;=\;s\,\inner{K}{M}.
\end{equation}
Consequently the \emph{Stein-trace norm}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:stein-norm-def}
  \calS_\nu(E):=\sup_{\norm M_\HS\le1}\abs{\ell_{\nu,E}(M)}^2
  \;=\;s^2\,\norm{K}_\HS^2 ,
\end{equation}
and the functional consumed by the geometric route is
$\calS_\nu(E)/s=s\norm K_\HS^2$ --- exactly the normalized covariance contrast of the Riccati
analysis of Section~\ref{sec:riccati}.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Write $m^E,m^F,\Sigma^E,\Sigma^F$ for the conditional means and covariances, so that
$p(m^E-a)+q(m^F-a)=0$ and, by the covariance decomposition \eqref{eq:cov-decomp},
$A=p\Sigma^E+q\Sigma^F+s\,\delta\delta^T$. Then
\[
  \int_E f_M\dd\nu
  =p\,\inner{M}{\Sigma^E+(m^E-a)(m^E-a)^T-A} .
\]
Now $\Sigma^E-A=q(\Sigma^E-\Sigma^F)-s\delta\delta^T=qG-s\delta\delta^T$ and, since
$m^E-a=q\delta$, $(m^E-a)(m^E-a)^T=q^2\delta\delta^T$. Hence
\[
  \int_E f_M\dd\nu
  =p\inner{M}{qG+(q^2-pq)\delta\delta^T}
  =s\inner{M}{G+(q-p)\delta\delta^T}=s\inner KM ,
\]
where we used $pq^2-p^2q=pq(q-p)$. The supremum over $\norm M_\HS\le1$ of the linear functional
$M\mapsto s\inner KM$ is $s\norm K_\HS$, giving \eqref{eq:stein-norm-def}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The identity $\calS_\nu(E)=s^2\norm K_\HS^2$ is a small but clarifying consolidation: the
``Stein'' functional of the geometric route and the matrix $K_t$ whose square drives the
Riccati source are literally the same translation-invariant object in two normalizations. The
two Eldan subroutes therefore exchange a single currency, and the conversion constants below are
universal on the tight window.
\end{remark}

\begin{lemma}[Conversion between Stein norm and Riccati source on the tight window]
\label{lem:stein-vs-source}
On the event $\{t<\tau_\eta\}$, with $S_t=s_t\norm{G_t}_\HS^2$ and $D_t\ge r_t^2$ as in
\eqref{eq:D-ge-r2},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:conversion}
  S_t\;\le\;2\,\frac{\calS_{\mu_t}(E)}{s_t}+64\,\eta^2 D_t,
  \qquad
  \frac{\calS_{\mu_t}(E)}{s_t}\;\le\;2\,S_t+64\,\eta^2 D_t .
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On the tight window $\abs{p_t-\tfrac12}\le\eta$, so $\abs{q_t-p_t}\le2\eta$ and
$s_t\ge\tfrac14-\eta^2\ge\tfrac3{16}$ for $\eta\le\tfrac14$; we only use $s_t\ge\tfrac14\cdot
(1-4\eta^2)\ge\tfrac1{4}\cdot\tfrac34$, and in fact only $r_t^2/s_t\le 8r_t^2$, valid for
$s_t\ge\tfrac18$. From $G=K-(q-p)\delta\delta^T$,
\[
  s\norm G_\HS^2\le2s\norm K_\HS^2+2s(q-p)^2\abs\delta^4
  \le2\,\frac{\calS}{s}+8\eta^2\,\frac{r^2}{s}
  \le2\,\frac{\calS}{s}+64\eta^2r^2 ,
\]
and $r^2\le D$ by \eqref{eq:D-ge-r2}. The reverse inequality is identical with the roles of $G$
and $K$ exchanged.
\end{proof}
