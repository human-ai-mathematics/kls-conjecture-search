\section{Definitions and normalization}
\label{sec:def}

Let $\Pstar$ be a probability measure on $\R^D$ with $\Pstar(\dd x)\propto e^{-U(x)}\dd x$
for a potential $U$, and let
\[
  L=\Delta-\nabla U\cdot\nabla
\]
be the generator of the associated overdamped Langevin diffusion
$\dd X_t=-\nabla U(X_t)\,\dd t+\sqrt2\,\dd B_t$, reversible with respect to $\Pstar$. The
three constants below are the objects of study throughout. The normalization is fixed once
and for all; changing it silently is a source of off-by-constant errors. All three have
units of length squared --- a smaller constant means a more tightly held-together measure.

\begin{definition}[Poincar\'e constant]
\label{def:poincare}
$\Pstar$ satisfies a Poincar\'e inequality with constant $\CP$ if
\begin{equation}\label{eq:poincare}
  \Var_{\Pstar}(f)\ \le\ \CP\,\E_{\Pstar}\norm{\nabla f}^2
  \qquad\text{for all smooth } f\in L^2(\Pstar).
\end{equation}
The optimal $\CP$ is the reciprocal of the spectral gap of $-L$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Log-Sobolev constant]
\label{def:lsi}
$\Pstar$ satisfies a logarithmic Sobolev inequality with constant $\CLS$ if
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lsi}
  \Ent_{\Pstar}(f^2)\ \le\ 2\,\CLS\,\E_{\Pstar}\norm{\nabla f}^2 ,
  \qquad
  \Ent_{\Pstar}(g)=\E_{\Pstar}\!\big[g\log g\big]-\E_{\Pstar}[g]\log\E_{\Pstar}[g].
\end{equation}
Equivalently, with $h=\dd Q/\dd\Pstar$ and the Fisher information
$I(Q\|\Pstar)=\int\norm{\nabla\log h}^2\dd Q$, the inequality reads
$\KL(Q\|\Pstar)\le\tfrac{\CLS}{2}\,I(Q\|\Pstar)$: entropy is controlled by infinitesimal
information.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Transportation-cost constant]
\label{def:tci}
$\Pstar$ satisfies the quadratic transportation-cost (Talagrand $T_2$) inequality with
constant $\CTCI$ if
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tci}
  \Wtwo^2(Q,\Pstar)\ \le\ 2\,\CTCI\,\KL(Q\,\|\,\Pstar)
  \qquad\text{for all } Q\ll\Pstar .
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

These constants govern convergence to equilibrium of the Langevin semigroup
$(P_t)_{t\ge0}=(e^{tL})_{t\ge0}$: a Poincar\'e inequality gives the $L^2$ decay
$\Var_{\Pstar}(P_tf)\le e^{-2t/\CP}\Var_{\Pstar}(f)$, and a log-Sobolev inequality the
stronger entropic decay $\KL(Q_t\|\Pstar)\le e^{-2t/\CLS}\KL(Q_0\|\Pstar)$. Thus $\CP$ is an
$L^2$-mixing time scale, $\CLS$ an entropy-mixing time scale, and (Definition~\ref{def:tci})
$\CTCI$ the constant converting an information-theoretic error $\KL(Q\|\Pstar)$ into a
geometric one $\Wtwo(Q,\Pstar)$ --- the figure of merit for Langevin sampling and for
variational inference \cite{VempalaWibisono2019,ChewiEtAl2020}.

The chosen normalization makes the two elementary comparisons constant-free.

\begin{theorem}[LSI implies Poincar\'e]
\label{thm:lsi-implies-poincare}
Under the normalization of Definitions~\ref{def:poincare}--\ref{def:lsi}, a log-Sobolev
inequality with constant $\CLS$ implies a Poincar\'e inequality with $\CP\le\CLS$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Apply \eqref{eq:lsi} to $f=1+\eps g$ with $g$ bounded and centred, and expand to second
order in $\eps$: $\Ent_{\Pstar}((1+\eps g)^2)=2\eps^2\Var_{\Pstar}(g)+o(\eps^2)$ while the
right-hand side is $2\CLS\eps^2\E_{\Pstar}\norm{\nabla g}^2+o(\eps^2)$. Dividing by
$2\eps^2$ and letting $\eps\to0$ gives \eqref{eq:poincare} with $\CP\le\CLS$
\cite{BakryGentilLedoux2014}.
\end{proof}

The transportation-cost comparison $\CTCI\le\CLS$ is the Otto--Villani theorem, recalled as
Theorem~\ref{thm:otto-villani}. Together,
\[
  \CP\ \le\ \CLS,
  \qquad
  \CTCI\ \le\ \CLS .
\]
A log-Sobolev bound is thus the strongest of the three, and an upper bound on $\CLS$
controls both the $W_2$-error (through entropy) and the spectral gap. The hierarchy
$\mathrm{LSI}\Rightarrow\{\text{Poincar\'e},\,T_2\}$ is strict: Poincar\'e alone gives
neither LSI nor $T_2$. We state each family at the strongest level it actually supports;
heavy-tailed families (Section~\ref{sec:tier4}) support no LSI and must be stated at the
Poincar\'e level only.
